Computer Science > Computation and Language [Submitted on 29 Aug 2025] Title:Not All Parameters Are Created Equal: Smart Isolation Boosts Fine-Tuning Performance View PDF HTML (experimental)Abstract:Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) is a pivotal approach to adapting large language models (LLMs) for downstream tasks; however, performance often suffers from the ``seesaw phenomenon'', where indiscriminate parameter updates yield progress on certain tasks at the expense of others. SENTENCE-LEVEL CONFIDENCE DYNAMICS AS A PROXY FOR REASONING QUALITY To understand the dynamics analyse the evolution of answer confidence across a reasoning chain. chain r composed of sentences (r!,7r?,... , 77”) and its cor- responding answer y, we measure how confidence in y changes as it processes more Reasoning Chains of the reasoning. We compute a sequence of scores, log p(y |r", x), for each partial reasoning pre- fix r'J, where j ranges from 1 to m. To capture the characteristics of these confidence sequences, we rank the responses by PiCSAR scoring function into three groups (highest, middle, and lowest), and analyse the “peakiness” the confidence trajectory within each group. We define a peak as a sentence where the confidence p(y | 714, a) exceeds the 95th percentile of all sentence-level scores observed across correctly answered samples for that problem. The peak-to-sentence ratio is this peak count by the total number of sentences. Table [4] reveals two key insights. (1) Higher peak-to-sentence ratio aligns with higher accuracy across differents models, showing that reasoning chains that lead to the correct answer tend have higher information density. For instance, Llama-3.1-8b achieves 53.33% accuracy with a 14.75% ratio in the highest-scoring group, compared to 44.20% with only 8.58% in the lowest. (2) Longer reasoning chains do not necessarily improve accuracy. Table|4|shows that lowest-ranked responses are substantially longer yet less accurate. For example, Llama-3.1-8b averages 64.72 sentences with 44.20% accuracy in the lowest group, versus 16.43 sentences with 53.33% in the highest group. Best-of-N (BoN) and Self-Consistency (SC). Log-likelihood has previously been known to fail as scoring function (Lewkowyez et al.| [2022), while PiCSAR contradicts its limitations by introducing additional answer confidence. C.1 THE IMPORTANCE OF SELECTION: INTERPRETING THE UPPER BOUND: While PiCSAR consistently outperforms other heuristics, it necessarily falls short of the oracle Up- per Bound, whose behaviour provides insight into the underlying challenges. On easier such as SVAMP and GSM8K, upper bound saturates quickly. For instance, increasing the sample size from 6 k = 32 with Llama-3.1-70b on GSMB8K raises accuracy only marginally from 96.91% to 97.44%, indicating correct reasoning paths are usually in small sample and that selection rather than generation is the main bottleneck. In contrast, on more demanding tasks such as MATH500 and GPQA-Diamond, upper bound continues to rise with larger k, as seen with on GPQA-Diamond where accuracy jumps from 55.22% to 82.49%, reflect- ing the intrinsic difficulty of generating correct answers. In both regimes, PiCSAR demonstrates its value: in selection-limited settings, it reliably identifies correct candidates from small pools, while in generation-limited scenarios, it narrows to the oracle reasoning even when answers are sparse, highlighting that improving selection is often as important as enlarging the sampling budget. 19 Reasoning Chains D_ ADDITIONAL EXPERIMENTS FOR CONFIDENCE SELECTION METHOD section, we show all the models across datasets (GSM8K, MATH500 and AIME2024), which consist of a variety of difficulties. The binary outcome variable encodes whether final answer is correct (y € 0,1), while the predictor is model’s confidence score expressed as the log-probability of final answer. This means Pr(y =1 | Conf) = o(a + 8 - Conf) where o is the sigmoid function. The regression coefficient 3 quantifies the change in log-odds per unit change in confidence. A positive 6 indicates that higher confidence increases likelihood of correctness.
